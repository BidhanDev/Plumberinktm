Water coming in right now? Do these four things first

This is the part that saves your floor, your ceiling and your bill. Do it while the phone is ringing.

1

Shut the water off

Close the main stopcock, or the outlet valve under the roof tank if you cannot find the stopcock. This stops the supply feeding the leak.

2

Switch off the pump

Turn the pump off at the board. A pump that keeps running against a closed line or a dry sump burns out within minutes.

3

Kill the power if water is near it

If water has reached a socket, a switchboard or a geyser, trip the main breaker before you step into the room.

4

Call and send a photo

Ring +977-9800000000 and send a photo on WhatsApp. We can often tell you the likely cause and the price on the call.

Plumbing services we do across Kathmandu Valley

Eight things make up almost every call we get. Each one has a rate band and a realistic time, so you know what you are agreeing to before we arrive.

Pipe leak repair

Damp patch on the wall, water bill climbing, or a ceiling stain spreading each week. We trace the line, open only the section we need, replace the damaged piece and reseal it. Most single joint leaks are done in one visit.

Rs 800 to Rs 3,000 typical 1 to 3 hours on site

Blocked drain and sewer cleaning

We clear the blockage with rods and a drain machine rather than pouring acid down the line, because acid eats the pipe and the clog comes back in a month. For repeat blockages we check the slope and the trap, which is usually the real problem.

Rs 1,500 to Rs 4,000 typical 1 to 2 hours on site

Bathroom and kitchen fitting

New fitting or a full bathroom redo. We set the correct fall on the waste line, seal every joint, and pressure test before we close the wall. We work with your tiler so the chasing and the tiling line up.

Rs 15,000 to Rs 60,000 typical 1 to 4 days on site

Water tank and pump work

Pump running dry, tank overflowing at 2 AM, or no pressure on the top floor. We fit float valves, foot valves, non return valves and automatic pump controllers, and we size the pump to the height of your building.

Rs 1,500 to Rs 7,000 typical 2 to 5 hours on site

Geyser installation and repair

A geyser needs a pressure release valve and a proper earth. We fit both, run the hot line in the right material, and check that the tank bracket can actually hold the weight when it is full.

Rs 2,500 to Rs 6,000 typical 2 to 4 hours on site

Tap, valve and mixer replacement

The small job most people put off. A dripping tap wastes a surprising amount of stored water, which matters when the tanker is your supply. We carry common sizes with us so it is usually one visit.

Rs 500 to Rs 1,500 typical 30 to 60 minutes on site

New building plumbing

We work from your drawing or we draw the layout ourselves, install the concealed lines before plastering, pressure test every run, and hand over a photo record of where each line sits inside the wall. That record saves you money on every future repair.

Reading the chart

Drain calls roughly triple in July and August, because monsoon water finds every weak joint and every half blocked line at the same time. Get your floor traps and roof drains cleared in May and you will probably skip that queue. Geyser failures cluster in December and January, when the element is working hardest. Pump and tank calls peak in April and May, when the supply is thin, tankers are busy, and pumps run dry more often than their makers ever intended.

Questions people ask before booking a plumber

If yours is not here, call and ask. We would rather answer it now than have you unhappy later.

How much does a plumber cost in Kathmandu?

For small work such as a tap change or a single joint leak, expect roughly NPR 500 to NPR 3,000 in labour. Drain cleaning with a machine runs about NPR 1,500 to NPR 4,000. A full bathroom fitting is quoted per job and usually sits between NPR 15,000 and NPR 60,000 depending on the fittings. We confirm the figure before starting, and the price on the bill is the price we quoted.

Do you have an emergency plumber available at night in Kathmandu?

Yes. Our line is answered 24 hours for burst pipes, overflowing tanks and blocked commodes. Night call out is NPR 1,000 for the visit, and the repair is charged on top at the normal rate. If we cannot reach you quickly we say so on the call rather than keeping you waiting.

Which areas of Kathmandu Valley do you cover?

All of Kathmandu, Lalitpur and Bhaktapur. We are based in New Baneshwor, so Baneshwor, Shankhamul, Tinkune, Koteshwor, Shantinagar and Sinamangal are usually reached within 20 minutes. Ring Road areas such as Baluwatar, Lazimpat, Thamel, Chabahil, Pulchowk and Jawalakhel take 25 to 30 minutes, and outer areas such as Bhaktapur, Budhanilkantha and Suryabinayak take 35 to 45 minutes.

What should I do while waiting for the plumber?

Close the stopcock or the tank outlet valve first, which stops more water entering the line. Switch off the pump at the board. If water is near any socket or the electric board, turn the main breaker off. Then move what you can off the floor and put a bucket under the drip. These four steps limit most of the damage.

Do you give a warranty on plumbing work?

Yes. Repairs carry a 6 month workmanship warranty and new fittings carry 12 months on our labour. If the same joint fails again inside that period, we come back and fix it at no charge. Manufacturer warranty on the fitting itself is separate and we hand you the bill for it.

Do you bring the materials or should I buy them?

Either way works. We carry common pipes, fittings, valves and taps in the van, and we show you the rate before fitting. If you prefer to buy your own, tell us the brand and size and we will fit what you supply, though the warranty then covers our labour only.

Can you work on a concealed line without breaking the whole wall?

In most cases yes. We trace the run first, then open only the section around the fault, usually a patch of about one foot. We cannot promise it for every situation, because old buildings often have lines that were never recorded, but we always try the smallest opening first.

My water pressure on the top floor is very low. Is that a pump problem?

Not always. In older Kathmandu houses it is often a rusted GI line that has narrowed from the inside, or a part blocked foot valve in the sump. We check the pump output first, then the line, so you do not end up buying a new pump you did not need.

Do you work on Saturdays and public holidays?

Yes. Normal booking hours are 7 AM to 8 PM every day of the week including Saturday. Dashain and Tihar days are emergency service only.

How do I book and how fast can you come?

Call or send a WhatsApp or Viber message with a photo of the problem and your area. We reply with a rough price and a time slot. Emergencies are attended the same day or the same night. Planned work such as bathroom fitting is usually booked 2 to 4 days ahead.
