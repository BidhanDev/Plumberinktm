Before you book

The four things people ask most on the phone. The full list is on the homepage FAQ.

How quickly can someone come?

Emergencies are attended the same day or the same night. Inside Ring Road we usually reach you in 20 to 30 minutes once a plumber is free. Planned work such as bathroom fitting is normally booked two to four days ahead, longer during monsoon when drain calls take over the schedule.

Will you tell me the price before starting?

Yes, always. You get a price band on the phone, then a firm figure once the plumber has seen the job. Nothing starts until you agree to that figure, and if the job turns out bigger than expected we stop and tell you rather than carrying on and adding it to the bill.

Is there a call out charge?

No charge for a daytime site visit inside Ring Road. Night emergency call out is NPR 1,000 for the visit, with the repair charged on top at the normal rate. Outside Ring Road we will tell you the travel charge, if any, on the call.

What should I have ready when I call?

Your area and nearest landmark, what you can see happening, roughly when it started, and whether you have already closed the water off. A photo on WhatsApp is worth more than all of it, because we can often identify the fitting and bring the right part first time.
